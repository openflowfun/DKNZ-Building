We are a licensed building company with 20+ years of experience in the construction industry, including over 10 years delivering high-quality projects across Canterbury and surrounding areas. Specialising in new builds, renovations, house levelling, foundation replacements, and complex earthquake repairs, we bring expert knowledge, exceptional attention to detail, and proven craftsmanship to every project.

From high-end kitchens and bathrooms to roofing, structural concreting, tiling, waterproofing, painting, and full interior/exterior finishes, we provide a complete end-to-end building service. Owner Dave personally leads every project with a strong focus on quality workmanship, efficient timelines, and competitive pricing — taking the pressure off you while saving time and money.
